linmath.h — a lean single-header column-major vector/matrix math library, used by
the desktop GUI's 3D spacetime overview (desktop/src/scene3d/, gui 10-T4) for its
mat4x4_perspective / mat4x4_look_at / mat4x4_mul camera math. Vendored at the
pinned commit recorded in scripts/third-party-digests.txt (name "linmath").

Upstream: https://github.com/datenwolf/linmath.h
